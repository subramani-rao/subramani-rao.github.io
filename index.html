About

Subramani Rao is a cybersecurity and product marketing leader specializing in go-to-market strategy, product positioning, and driving adoption for cutting-edge security and cyber resilience solutions. He focuses on bridging cybersecurity and data protection to help organizations stay resilient against evolving threats, with particular depth in ransomware resilience and operational technology (OT) security. He is the author of two books on AI and cybersecurity, a CISSP-certified security professional, and a recognized voice in industry press and at international conferences.
