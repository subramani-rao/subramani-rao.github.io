About

Hey, I'm Subramani 👋

GTIA Award, 2026

I'm a cybersecurity leader specializing in GRC and compliance, SOC transformation, and security posture management. My work spans risk analysis, threat assessment, and business impact analysis - building risk scoring frameworks that give leadership clear visibility into exposure, and leading incident response across complex, global organizations. Over my career I've worked across a number of leading cybersecurity and technology vendors, and led critical cybersecurity projects for some of the world's best-known organizations.

My client work spans British Telecom, Sky, Vodafone, and several leading banks and retail companies across the UK and worldwide - helping them strengthen resilience against ransomware, secure operational technology (OT) environments, and stay ahead of evolving threats.

British Telecom Sky Vodafone Leading UK & Global Banks Retail Enterprises Worldwide

Alongside my vendor and enterprise work, I also bring consulting experience advising venture capital firms - providing cybersecurity and technology investment guidance to help investors evaluate emerging security technologies and de-risk their portfolio decisions.

I'm the author of two books on AI and cybersecurity, a CISSP-certified security professional, and a recognized voice in industry press and at international conferences.
